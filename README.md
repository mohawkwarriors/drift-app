## 🌟 Overview

**Drift** is a distraction-free, zero-dependency breathing app crafted with organic fluid physics and frosted glassmorphism. It uses a clinically proven **16-second box breathing cadence** (4s Inhale, 4s Hold, 4s Exhale, 4s Hold) to help regulate the nervous system, lower resting heart rate, and clear mental tension.

Drift features a living, liquid-wax organism that ebbs and flows in harmony with your breath—rising and expanding on the inhale, and sinking deep into relaxation on the exhale.

## ✨ Features

- 💧 **Organic Liquid Organism**: Built with pure CSS multi-axis fluid morphing and harmonic wave scaling. No rigid timers or jarring jumps.
- 🧘 **Ink-Bleed Phase Counter**: Direction-aware numerical transitions that float like ink in water, synchronized perfectly with the breath cycle.
- ☀️ **Dual Sunrise & Bedtime Themes**:
  - **Morning Mode**: Dawn-inspired golden apricot, morning coral, and warm sky light to spark mindful alertness.
  - **Night Mode**: Low-stimulus, warm celestial violet and deep indigo palette designed to protect melatonin production before sleep.
- ⏱️ **Customizable Duration Presets**:
  - **Brief**: Fast 1-minute reset (4 breath cycles).
  - **Reset**: 2-minute restorative pause (8 breath cycles).
  - **Deep**: 4-minute full nervous system reset (15 breath cycles).
  - *Fully configurable from 1 to 5 minutes in Settings.*
- 🫧 **Cycle Tracker**: Minimalist frosted droplet indicators at the top that breathe alongside you and gently pop to full volume upon completing each cycle.
- 🔔 **Morning & Evening Reminders**: In-app scheduling with Web Notification API support for gentle daily morning and wind-down prompts.
- 📳 **Haptic Feedback**: Soft, physical vibration pulses signaling phase transitions on supported mobile devices.
- 💾 **Local Persistence**: Tracks your mindful breathing streaks and saves custom preferences automatically to your browser’s `localStorage`.
- 📱 **Mobile-First & PWA-Ready**: Full edge-to-edge layout (`100dvh`), iOS/Android notch protection, and zero pull-to-refresh interference.
